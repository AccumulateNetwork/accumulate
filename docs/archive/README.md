## Archive Policy

Documents are archived when:
1. They are superseded by newer approaches
2. The implementation strategy has fundamentally changed
3. They are no longer actively maintained
4. They contain outdated technical information

Archived documents are preserved for:
- Historical reference
- Understanding evolution of approaches
- Recovery of useful patterns or insights
- Compliance and audit purposes
